## Launch plan (from the video's advice)

1. **List the Christmas Survival Guide and the Christmas puzzle pack first.** People are planning Christmas on Pinterest right now.
2. **Make 3–4 pins per product**, each with a different angle (problem, inside pages, result, gift). The pin titles above are written that way.
3. **Post about 8 pins a day** across all products, and the same link no more than once a week.
4. **Use the free samples as email sign-up gifts.** Add them to MailerLite as new freebies, so each new product launches to people who already know you.
5. **Keep pinning week after week.** Products sell because of the pins you keep adding, not one launch post.
6. **Ads later:** once a pin gets clicks for free, try it as an ad at about £10 a day with three images, aimed at Ireland, the Netherlands, Sweden and New Zealand before the US.

## Before you publish: final checks

- Read every page once, as the video suggests. You know your customers best, so change anything that doesn't sound like you.
- Low-Spoon Kitchen: the recipes use standard UK methods and times, but try a couple yourself if you can.
- Money Reset: the support organisations listed are real UK services. Check their websites are still current before each new season.
- Puzzle packs: every sudoku is checked by the generator to have exactly one solution, and every word search word appears exactly once.
