## Before you list

- **Upload the zip**, not the loose files. Etsy allows up to 5 files of 20MB each per digital listing; every zip here is under 15MB.
- **Listing photos:** upload `etsy-1.jpg` first, then the rest in order. Etsy allows 10 photos, so you have room for lifestyle photos made from the prompts below.
- **AI disclosure:** Etsy asks sellers to be open about how items are made. These products were designed with AI assistance, so check Etsy's current creativity standards and add the disclosure it asks for. A line such as *"Designed by Larkspur & Co. with the help of AI tools"* at the end of each description is a simple, honest option.
- **Canva:** the Word and PowerPoint templates can be imported into Canva (Create a design › Import file). If you ever sell Canva versions, share them as template links and only use free Canva elements or your own artwork.
- **Fees and rates change.** The Etsy fee defaults (checked October 2026) and the 2026/27 UK tax rates are editable in the spreadsheets. Re-check them each April and whenever Etsy announces a change.
- **Bundles vs single listings:** keep the individual listings live too. Bundles work best linked from each single listing ("Save over 50% with the Busy Brain Collection").
