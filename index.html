Spoon theory, explained

"I don't have the spoons" is one of the best ways to explain energy. This three minute video explains where spoon theory came from, how it fits ADHD and long term illness, and how to use it day to day.

Spoon theory was created by Christine Miserandino in 2003. It is an analogy, not a diagnosis or medical advice.
